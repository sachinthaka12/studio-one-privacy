Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Studio One respects the privacy of our customers. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when customers communicate with Studio One through WhatsApp and our automated customer support services.

2. Information We May Receive

When you contact Studio One through WhatsApp, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide, such as your WhatsApp phone number, profile name, messages, enquiries, order-related information, and other information you choose to send to us.

3. How We Use Information

Information received through WhatsApp may be used to respond to customer enquiries, provide information about our products and services, assist with orders and customer support, and improve our customer service.

4. Automated and AI-Assisted Responses

Studio One may use automated systems and artificial intelligence services to help process customer enquiries and generate responses. Information necessary to answer a request may therefore be processed by service providers used to operate these features.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell customer personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate our messaging and customer support systems, subject to their applicable privacy and security practices.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for customer support, order administration, business records, security, or other legitimate business purposes, subject to applicable law.

7. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your interactions with Studio One by contacting us through our official WhatsApp number and clearly stating that you would like your personal information deleted.

After receiving a valid deletion request, we will review the request and delete applicable information from systems under our control, except where retention is required for legal, security, accounting, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

8. Third-Party Services

Our WhatsApp customer support system may rely on third-party services, including WhatsApp/Meta and artificial intelligence or hosting providers. Their processing of information is governed by their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request deletion of your information, please contact Studio One through our official WhatsApp contact.
